How To Use
Drag players on the stage to reposition.
Right-click + drag to orbit the camera.
Scroll to zoom in/out.
Middle-click + drag to pan.

The heatmap shows mic sensitivity — green is the sweet spot, red is weak pickup.

Instrument directivity is modeled: each instrument radiates sound differently based on its body shape. The arrow on each player shows their facing direction, which affects both voice projection and instrument radiation.

Tip: Use a phone dB meter app (e.g. NIOSH SLM, Decibel X) to measure each player at 1 meter. Enter those values in the SPL sliders.
