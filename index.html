How To Use
Drag players to reposition on stage.
Drag arrow tips to aim voice or instrument independently.
Drag source markers (spheres) up/down to adjust source height.
Right-click drag to orbit camera. Scroll to zoom.

The heatmap shows mic sensitivity. Auto Balance optimizes positions for even mix.

Tip: Use a phone dB meter (NIOSH SLM, Decibel X) to measure each player at 1m.
