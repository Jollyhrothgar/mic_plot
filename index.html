How To Use
Drag players to reposition on stage.
Click+drag Vox arrow to aim singing direction.
Click+drag Inst arrow to aim instrument direction.
Drag source cubes up/down to adjust source height.
Click a player to show directivity patterns.
Right-click drag to orbit camera. Scroll to zoom.

The heatmap shows mic sensitivity on the floor. The cardioid outlines show the mic's pickup pattern (green=strong, yellow=moderate, red=weak).

Tip: Use a phone dB meter (NIOSH SLM, Decibel X) to measure each player at 1m.
